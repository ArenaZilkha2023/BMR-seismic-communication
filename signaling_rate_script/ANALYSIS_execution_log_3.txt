
*** Analysis workflow ***
=======================================================
1. Temporal analysis of signaling --> time_bin_analysis.m

This analysis is independent of the spatial beam-analysis pipeline.
The script processes the head-drum data from each experimental session and divides the recording into three temporal periods:
- early
- middle
- late

The resulting data are stored as MATLAB structures from which head-drum counts and signal amplitudes are extracted.
Statistical analyses of signaling rate and amplitude were subsequently performed in GraphPad Prism, and the corresponding
manuscript figures were generated in Prism. Therefore, the statistical testing and final figure generation for this component
are not performed by the script.
