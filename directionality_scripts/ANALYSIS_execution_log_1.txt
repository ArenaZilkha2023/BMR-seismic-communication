
*** Analysis workflow ***
=======================================================
1. Seismic propagation and attenuation --> BMR_project.m

This script analyzes the geophone recordings to characterize seismic propagation through the experimental arena.
It was used to estimate:

-seismic signal propagation speed, approximately 85.5 m/s
-seismic attenuation with distance, approximately −14 dB/m

These estimates were subsequently used during signal matching and source localization using the previously developed
Batalef MATLAB package.

---------------------------------------------------------
2. Beam-width analysis --> compute_cross_beam_section.m

This script takes the interpolated amplitude matrices generated by the kriging analysis and estimates seismic beam width.

For each reconstructed seismic beam, the script identifies the amplitude maximum and calculates an amplitude cross-section 
perpendicular to the vector connecting the estimated BMR position with the beam maximum. Beam width is estimated from the 
points at which amplitude decreases by 6 dB relative to the peak.

The script also generates the beam cross-section visualization presented in the manuscript.
